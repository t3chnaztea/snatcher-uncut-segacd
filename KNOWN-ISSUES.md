# Known issues and compatibility

The patch uses a matching USA Sega CD dump. Wrong regions, revisions, ISO/2048 data and already-patched sources are rejected. Extract CHD input first. The browser supports single-BIN CUE or raw Track 1. Split tracks and virtual PREGAP use the offline installer.

Japanese chronology retains the unchanged English narration, including spoken 1996 beside restored 1991 text. Original Sega CD dates avoid that chronology mismatch. Fine-tuning individual date controls can create a mixed timeline; the tool marks it.

Wide-scene costumes are separate from the five interactive guest groups. Each guest choice keeps its rear/front views, dialogue and headings together. H2O/Gulliver share one branch. Miss Money's two views share one choice. Reference comparisons show the original and complete restored versions; mixed choices can look different.

The patch is a partial restoration. Most localization, expanded Act 3, voices, music and adult ages remain. Previously excluded interactions and age changes remain excluded. Plato's Cavern stays because the English dialogue uses that name.

Default output is identical to the earlier accepted game. Its emulator ending, credits and normal save/reload evidence still applies. It worked on a U.S. Genesis Model 2 with a Mega EverDrive Pro. That report does not establish a full console playthrough or console save/load acceptance. Other configurations have no console acceptance.

Fresh guest-view checks use documented graphics QA prefixes. They establish rendering, resource use and bound text pools. They do not establish a natural story route or visible dialogue for every configuration.

Build and browser testing use macOS. Playwright WebKit results do not establish Safari application compatibility. Windows, Linux and mobile installation are not accepted. Desktop and narrow-screen interface checks cover layout and controls only.

Raw Track 1 alone is not playable. Keep the original audio and a matching CUE. Virtual PREGAP can preserve supplied audio and timing, not samples absent from your dump. BIN identification checks Track 1 only.

Keep originals and back up your saves. New combinations do not claim new save/load, CHD or Mega EverDrive package acceptance.
