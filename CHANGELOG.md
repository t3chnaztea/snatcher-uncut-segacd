## v0.9.0-rc1

Initial release candidate. Physical-console acceptance has not run.

The restoration comprises 37 approved records. There are 31 text records: 26 chronology records and five cameo identity groups. There are six art records: three opening date captions and three visual restoration families. A record can cover several dependent edits or views.

### Text

Selected dates return the main chronology to 2042. Dependent date edits follow the compared source records. The text changes comprise 72 encoded edits across dialogue and headings. Most of the English localization remains intact.

Outer Heaven descriptions and headings identify the restored costume patrons. Short resident headings use "Corn." and "Money" where space is limited. Associated text gives Cornelius and Miss Money in full. The existing combined H2O/Gulliver menu branch remains.

### Art

Outer Heaven uses the six source costume views in its rear and front views. The original dancer and lighting animation remain.

The dog scene uses the restored 14-frame animation with its completion wait. The surrounding scene and original audio remain. The adult corpse scene uses the approved source pose.

The opening captions show 1991 for Moscow, 2039 on both newspaper date elements, and 2042 for Neo Kobe. Shared font glyphs remain unchanged.

### Preserved content and limits

The English voice cast, original music, and Sega CD story additions remain. Adult ages remain 18. Optional menus and other unapproved changes remain unchanged. Excluded sexualized-minor content and excluded age changes remain unchanged.

The opening and database show 1991 for the catastrophe date. Retained speech says 1996. The patch does not alter audio to resolve this discrepancy.

This release contains no game image or BIOS. The release patch applies to the supported raw data track. Audio comes from the player's own dump.
